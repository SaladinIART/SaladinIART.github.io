## Public Safety Boundaries

- Do not name the current employer, employment agency, customers, or internal
  products on the public website.
- Do not publish employee IDs, production figures, schedules, drawings, SOP/WI
  details, real tube dimensions, or identifiable workplace observations.
- The tube-preparation concept must use invented or sanitized inputs, state that
  it is unvalidated, and state that it is unaffiliated with any employer.
- The private tube-concept source repository is not a public portfolio link. Its
  public-safe website diagrams omit numerical assumptions and build geometry.
- The AMR Process Lab remains private with no source or interactive demo link
  until its later evidence and publication gate is separately approved.
- Mark every project honestly as deployed, validated demo, prototype,
  engineering concept, or in development.
- Do not add a Factory Energy repository link until its public README has a
  separate privacy review; it currently exposes figures intentionally omitted
  from this portfolio.
- Do not restore pricing, booking, sales, response-time, client-capacity, or
  active-services language.
